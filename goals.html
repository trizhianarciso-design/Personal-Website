My Goals as a Student

Here are the things I’m actually working toward. (Tap a card to read more.)

Short-Term Goals

Finish college on time and level up my IT skills.

I want to graduate on time without delays. At the same time, I’m focused on improving my web development, coding, and overall IT skills so I become job-ready as soon as possible.

Career Goals

Land a good job after graduation.

My main goal is to find a stable job in IT or web development. I want to start earning so I can support myself and help provide for my family.

Financial Goals

Earn, save, invest, and buy the things I want.

I want to make enough money to buy the things I’ve always wanted, help my family, start investing early, and slowly build wealth. Getting rich is the long-term dream, but it starts with smart money habits now.

Personal Goals

Grow as a person while chasing success.

I want to become more disciplined, improve my skills continuously, and build a life where I can enjoy the things I love — while still working hard for my future and my family.
